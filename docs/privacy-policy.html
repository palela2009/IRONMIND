IRONMIND Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

IRONMIND is an Android app that helps you reduce time spent on distracting apps. This policy explains exactly what data the app collects, why, and who it is shared with.

Who is responsible

IRONMIND is operated by the app's developer, contactable at ironmind.official.app@gmail.com.

What we collect

DataWhy
Email address and display name To create and identify your account, and to show your name to friends you connect with.
Profile photo (only if you upload one) Shown on your profile and to friends on the leaderboard.
App usage data — which of your selected apps you open, and for how long This is the core function of IRONMIND. It is used to trigger challenges, enforce the daily limits you set, show your screen-time statistics, and settle duels with friends.
Challenge results — whether you exited in time, and how quickly To calculate your streak, level, statistics and reaction-time history.
Friend connections To show the leaderboard and run duels between people who have added each other.
Notification token To send you notifications about duels and leaderboard changes.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect the contents of any app — no messages, posts, photos, browsing history or keystrokes. IRONMIND only knows which app is open and for how long.
  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not collect your contacts.
  • We do not sell your data to anyone, ever.

Usage Access permission

IRONMIND requires Android's Usage Access permission. This lets the app see which application is currently in the foreground and how long apps have been used. It is the only way an Android app can detect that you have opened a distracting app, and IRONMIND cannot function without it. This permission does not give the app access to anything inside those apps.

Who your data is shared with

We use the following services to run IRONMIND. Each receives only what it needs:

ServiceWhat it handles
Google Firebase AuthenticationSign-in, and your email address
MongoDB AtlasStores your account, statistics, screen-time totals and friends
RenderHosts the server that the app talks to
Expo Push NotificationsDelivers notifications to your device
Google AdMobShows optional rewarded video ads, if you choose to watch one
SentryReceives a report when the app crashes, so we can fix it. Reports contain the error and the app version, and an anonymous account id - never your email, name or photo

Other IRONMIND users can see your display name, profile photo, streak, level, rank and badges — but only people you have added as friends, and only via the leaderboard and duels. Your screen-time totals are never shown to other users; duels compare only the minutes for the single app being contested.

Advertising

IRONMIND shows rewarded video ads only when you actively choose to watch one, for example to recover a lost streak. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may use a device advertising identifier. We request non-personalised ads. You can read Google's policy at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

How long we keep your data

Your data is kept while your account exists. Deleting your account removes it.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete Account inside the app. This permanently deletes your account, statistics, challenge history, screen-time records, friend connections and duels from our database, and removes your sign-in record. It cannot be undone. You can also email us at the address above to request deletion.

Children

IRONMIND is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted, by emailing ironmind.official.app@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be posted here with a new date at the top.
