# IRONMIND Play Store phone screenshots

Final English promotional images, generated and edited with the built-in image_gen tool on 2 November 2026.

Upload these four PNGs to Main store listing > Graphics > Phone screenshots, in this order:

1. `01-home.png` - overall screen time, tracked-app usage and challenge progress.
2. `02-friends.png` - the friends leaderboard with realistic ranked times.
3. `03-invite-and-duel.png` - invitations, referral rewards and duel history.
4. `04-profile.png` - profile statistics, XP, streaks and achievements.

All four images are 941 x 1672 pixels, 24-bit RGB PNG without transparency. These satisfy Google's basic phone screenshot format, dimension and aspect-ratio requirements. Google determines approval and promotional eligibility separately.

The screenshots use illustrative demo data. Personal emails, names, photographs and invite codes were replaced with fictional names, initials avatars, alex@example.com and DEMO42. Home's tracked usage totals 2 hours (YouTube 1 hour, Instagram 40 minutes, TikTok 20 minutes), matching the leaderboard's You entry. Overall phone usage is 5 hours 41 minutes. Lower tracked-app time ranks higher on the leaderboard.

The three outlined icons were removed from the duel-history artwork at the user's request. This changes only these marketing images, not the installed app. The Home headline is now "See where your time goes. Take back your time."

The complete generation and revision prompts are in `prompts.json`. Original screenshots and generated drafts were left untouched.

Google's requirements: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151?hl=en
